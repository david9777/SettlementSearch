# Evergreen Campaigns — Master List

> Running list of possible **evergreen** website/funnel ideas to consider building.
> Evergreen = an "always-on" intake funnel tied to a recurring/ongoing legal claim type
> (not a one-off settlement). Build the site + workflow once, then run ads to it
> continuously and feed qualified leads into employment (or other) cases.
>
> Nothing here is committed. This is a parking lot to review and discuss before we
> decide what (if anything) to move on.

## 1. Competitor / model to watch: Shavitz Law Group

**Why on the list:** Gregg Shavitz / Shavitz Law Group (Boca Raton, FL) is an AV-rated
boutique that does **wage-and-hour only** and has been running this intake machine for
years (claims ~$400M recovered). They co-counsel with the heavyweights — Morgan & Morgan,
Werman Salas, Berger Montague. This is the playbook to reverse-engineer.

### The model, in one line
**One evergreen master site (shavitzlaw.com) + a swarm of single-purpose microsites,
one domain per employer/claim**, each pointing at the same phone number and the same
intake form. Build the chassis once; spin up a new `{employer}overtime.com` per target.

### Microsite network (the part to copy)
Each is a dedicated domain, near-identical template, different employer/job title:

| Microsite | Target employer | Job title targeted |
|---|---|---|
| [publixovertime.com](https://publixovertime.com/) | Publix | Assistant Department Managers |
| [gapovertime.com](https://gapovertime.com/) | Gap / Banana Republic / Athleta | Assistant Store Managers |
| [gallagherovertime.com](https://gallagherovertime.com/) | Arthur J. Gallagher | Client Service Associates |
| [bbgovertime.com](https://bbgovertime.com/) | BBG (appraisal) | Appraisers / Client Service Assoc. |
| [rsuovertime.com](https://rsuovertime.com/) | Any employer paying in RSUs | Employees whose RSU comp wasn't counted toward OT |

> Naming convention is dead simple: **`{employer}overtime.com`**. Easy to guess/scout —
> probe other big employers' names to find ones already parked or live.

### Their intake-form template (verbatim field list, from gallagherovertime.com)
Reusable as our own form spec:
- First / Last name
- Email
- Phone
- State where worked
- Job title(s)
- Dates of employment + End date
- Message (free text)
- Consent checkbox (attorney–client disclaimer)

**CTAs:** primary *"Click Here To Contact Us For a Free Consultation"* + a persistent
*"Call Us For a Free Consultation (800) 616-4000."* Phone-first, form-second.

### Their pitch + urgency formula (copy this structure)
- **Hook:** "{Employer} {job title} — you may be owed unpaid overtime."
- **Proof of claim:** concrete off-the-clock examples — working through unpaid lunch,
  answering calls/email on days off, pre-shift/post-shift work due to labor-budget caps.
- **Urgency 1 — statute of limitations:** back-pay window of **3 yrs (federal) / 4 yrs CA /
  6 yrs NY**; "the clock is running, every day you wait you lose a day of back pay."
- **Urgency 2 — certification deadline:** "a motion for conditional certification is
  pending; join *before* the court authorizes notice."
- **Risk reversal:** free consultation, no cost to you.

### Case roster (shows the cadence of new campaigns — ~one new target every few weeks)
Bloomberg (Jun 2025) · Avis managers misclassification (Apr 2025) · Teva sales $2.7M
(Oct 2024) · Stryker · Hertz managers · Apple (stock-comp/OT) · Starbucks $2.9M
off-the-clock · MyEyeDr store managers · GEICO meal-break $155K.

### Takeaways for us
1. **Microsite-per-claim on a shared chassis** — the single biggest lesson. One template,
   swap employer + job title + dates.
2. **Phone-first intake** with a tiny qualifying form (the 8 fields above).
3. **Two-layer urgency** (statute of limitations *and* certification deadline).
4. **Co-counsel model** — Shavitz sources the leads, partners with big firms to litigate.
   Maps directly to a referral-fee monetization for us.

## 2. 1099 worker overtime / misclassification funnel  ⭐ (lead candidate)

**The angle:** Target **1099 / "independent contractor" workers** and screen whether they're
actually misclassified employees owed overtime under the FLSA (and state wage laws). Many
1099 workers are economically dependent employees and are owed OT, minimum wage, and
expense reimbursement.

**Who we'd target:** Anyone paid on a 1099 who works set hours/under company control —
delivery & courier drivers, gig/last-mile, installers/techs, sales reps, drivers,
construction labor, healthcare staffing, etc.

**Why it's a good evergreen base:**
- Huge, always-refreshing pool of workers (misclassification is ongoing).
- The **same funnel + workflow generalizes** to other employment claims (unpaid OT,
  off-the-clock work, exempt misclassification, meal/rest breaks, tips, etc.) — build
  the chassis once, swap the angle/landing page per campaign.

**What we'd build:**
- Evergreen landing site: "Paid on a 1099? You may be owed overtime."
- Short qualifying intake (hours/week, how paid, who controls the work, employer, state).
- Workflow to route qualified leads → intake → referral/co-counsel or our own case.
