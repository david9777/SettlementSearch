# NC + MD BCBA Roster — Legitimate Data Routes

Three compliant ways to build and work the list, in place of scraping the
verification portals (which prohibit bulk/commercial use).

---

## 1. Public-records request — NC Behavior Analyst Licensure Board

Send to the board's contact listed at https://ncbehavioranalystboard.org
(confirm the current records-request address/email on the site).

> **Subject: Public Records Request — Licensee Roster (N.C. Gen. Stat. Ch. 132)**
>
> To the Custodian of Public Records:
>
> Under the North Carolina Public Records Law (G.S. Chapter 132), I request a
> list of all persons currently licensed by the Board as Licensed Behavior
> Analysts (LBA) and Licensed Assistant Behavior Analysts (LABA), including for
> each: full name, license number, license type, license status, original date
> of licensure, and the city/county and business practice location on file.
>
> Please provide the records in electronic format (CSV or Excel) if available.
> I understand a reasonable fee may apply for compilation; please advise of any
> cost before processing. Please also let me know of any restrictions the Board
> places on the use of this list.
>
> Thank you,
> [Name / L&K / contact]

## 2. Public-records request — Maryland Board of Professional Counselors & Therapists (BOPC)

Board contact: (410) 764-4732; confirm current mailing address / PIA contact at
https://health.maryland.gov/bopc/Pages/index.aspx

> **Subject: Public Information Act Request — Behavior Analyst Licensee List**
>
> To the PIA Custodian, Board of Professional Counselors & Therapists:
>
> Under the Maryland Public Information Act (Md. Code, General Provisions
> §§ 4-101 et seq.), I request a list of all persons currently licensed as
> Behavior Analysts and Assistant Behavior Analysts, including for each: full
> name, license number, license type, license status, original effective/issue
> date, and the city and business location on file.
>
> Please provide the records in electronic format (CSV or Excel) if available.
> I understand a reasonable fee may apply; please advise before processing, and
> note any restrictions on use of the list.
>
> Thank you,
> [Name / L&K / contact]

## 3. Outreach — BACB Mass Email Service

The BACB registry may **not** be used for solicitation, but the BACB's official
Mass Email Service exists specifically to send certificants employment
opportunities. Submit a campaign here:
https://www.bacb.com/mass-email-information/ — this is the compliant way to put
your roles in front of NC/MD BCBAs.

---

### What these fields cover (and don't)
Board records and the BACB registry give the professional fields — name,
credential, license #, issue date (→ years licensed), status, city, practice
location. They do **not** include home address, age, or personal phone/email;
boards redact those, which is why the tracker doesn't have those columns.

### Note on use restrictions
Some boards restrict using licensee lists for commercial solicitation even when
the list is a public record. Confirm permitted use with each board, and be
candid about the purpose when you request it.
